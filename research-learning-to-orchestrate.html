Research direction · 03

Learning to orchestrate.

Using deep and multi-agent reinforcement learning to make adaptive resource-orchestration decisions under changing demand and system state.

The central question

How can an orchestration policy learn effective resource decisions as users, demand, and infrastructure conditions change?

01 · Motivation

Why learning?

Exact optimization provides valuable benchmarks but can become too slow for large, dynamic systems. Reinforcement learning offers a way to learn reusable policies from repeated interaction with a simulated environment.

02 · Rendering farms

Parallel placement

I developed learning-based orchestration for distributed in-network rendering farms, where agents coordinate service placement and resource allocation across heterogeneous nodes to reduce latency and increase accepted users.

03 · Method

Experimental pipeline

  • Custom Gymnasium environments and RLlib training.
  • PPO-family algorithms and multi-objective rewards.
  • MILP, heuristic, and learning baselines.
  • Evaluation across changing user loads and network states.

Research outcome

The learned orchestration strategies achieved low average latency at scale while maintaining high service acceptance.
